In 2022, I took a year off to travel and explore life without a pager.

One of the projects I started was BIG PIT, with members of local Toronto/GTA
bands [Sarin](https://sarin.bandcamp.com/) (now sadly defunct) and [Teleportoise](https://teleportoise.bandcamp.com/).

We played [POST Festival](https://www.thepostfestival.com/) in Indiana and a
bunch of local and not so local shows, then released an album.

It turns out you can just e-mail producers and engineers who have worked on
albums you grew up on, and they will work on your music for an appropriate day
rate.

We ended up getting the album mixed by [Matt
Bayles](https://www.mattbayles.com/), of Mastadon, Isis, Botch, Pelican, Russian
Circles, and Sumac fame (well, famous if you're a recording nerd like I am).

We chose to self-release, and managed to recoup our costs through album sales
and playing shows within about a year, which to this day surprises me. I still
have several boxes of vinyl kicking around, as we had planned a 10-date US
release tour, which got cut down to 2 shows because my US artist visa didn't
turn up in time.
