In 2022, I took a year off to travel and explore life without a pager.

One of the projects I started was BIG PIT, with members of local Toronto/GTA
bands [Sarin](https://sarin.bandcamp.com/) (now sadly defunct) and [Teleportoise](https://teleportoise.bandcamp.com/).

We played [POST Festival](https://www.thepostfestival.com/) in Indiana and a
bunch of local and not so local shows, then released an album. Pretty good use
of a year off in my opinion!
